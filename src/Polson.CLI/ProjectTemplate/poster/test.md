## What this workflow tests

`poster` is the first workflow whose design rests on **measured audience taste**: the `Taste` tool
(Qloo), a `direction.md` that cites evidence line by line, and the print file with its checks
(`outPdf`). Reach for `polson://manual/14` §3a for the print file, `polson://sdk/core/Taste` for the
evidence, and `polson://manual/09` and `/11` for composition and type.

Answer these in `findings.md`:

- **Did the profile separate the audiences?** How many distinctive references did each get, how much
  did they overlap, and were they the kind of thing you could design from? Name any that were wrong for
  the audience, and say how you could tell.
- **What did the evidence change?** For each poster, name two choices that came from Qloo and would have
  been different without it. If you cannot, say so — that is the finding this workflow most needs.
- **Where did the evidence run out?** Which choices had no Qloo or research behind them, and how did you
  decide them?
- **Was `Taste` usable as described?** Was `profile` the right first call; did `locality` place each
  audience correctly; did anything get refused, and was the refusal useful? Requests spent, from `status`.
- **The print checks.** Which `[WARN]` lines fired, which did you fix, and which did you keep and why?
  Were any of them wrong?
- **The distance renders.** Did the far render change what you did? Was the arithmetic in the
  instructions enough to make one, or did you have to work it out?
- **The director's questions.** Which did you ask, which did you assume, and was the list the right one?
- **The concepts.** Were your three per audience genuinely different — dominant element, composition,
  palette — or one idea three ways? Which item in the profile started each, and why did the chosen one
  win?
- **A product ad** (type `product`). What did each comparable resolve to, and did the buyers' local-only lists
  give you anything to design from? The packshot: one generation, the same can in every view, no stray
  lettering, and did your label read on it? Quote the claim as printed. Qloo requests spent, from `status`.
- **The set.** Could a stranger tell the posters were for different people — and match each to its
  audience? With more than two, which two came closest, and were their audiences close too?
